# Lone Star Academy OS — regulatory source map (for verification, not automated certification)

Research performed 2026-10-14. The sources below are published government guidance/rules. **A campus manager must verify their current licenses, school-approved curricula, enrollment contracts, training location, and state-specific implementation with the appropriate state authority before changing graduation rules or declaring compliance.** This document does not establish that any campus is licensed or that any student has met a requirement.

## Federal ELDT and Training Provider Registry
- Official FMCSA CDL process: https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/registration/commercial-drivers-license/how-do-i-get-commercial-drivers-license
- Official TPR driver training FAQ: https://tpr.fmcsa.dot.gov/FAQ/Topics/drivers%2Ctraining-requirements
- Official provider FAQ: https://tpr.fmcsa.dot.gov/FAQ/Topics/provider-requirements
- **CLP:** FMCSA states that federal law does **not** require a driver to finish ELDT theory before applying for a commercial learner permit. States may have additional rules; confirm NCDMV and Texas DPS instructions for knowledge-test scheduling and permit issuance.
- **CDL skills:** A first-time Class A/B applicant must have applicable theory and range/public-road behind-the-wheel completion certified to FMCSA TPR before the CDL skills test may be administered. Hazardous materials theory completion precedes the H endorsement knowledge test.
- Training providers must electronically submit successful completion to FMCSA TPR by midnight of the **second business day** following completion under 49 CFR 380.717, as explained in the FMCSA provider FAQ.
- Academy OS currently **tracks** external submission and confirmation details; it does **not** automatically submit to FMCSA. A tracking entry marked Submitted externally is not proof until the actual provider portal/TPR confirms.

## North Carolina (Greensboro campus): 19A NCAC 03J
- Official North Carolina Rules Review approved/readopted provisions: https://files.nc.gov/oah/documents/files/Volume-36-Issue-01-July-1-2021_0.pdf
- NCDOT published course text: https://www.ncdot.gov/about-us/how-we-operate/policy-process/rules/Documents/19a-ncac-03j-0306.pdf
- Rule .0306 describes the commercial full course minimum: **50 hours classroom including testing, 50 hours field, 20 hours highway behind the wheel as the student driver, 40 hours highway observation = 160 hours**, in at least four calendar weeks. It includes **three hours of highway behind-the-wheel student driving between dusk and dawn**, school Driver's Daily Logs and vehicle/student occupancy limits. Do not count observation as actual driving, duplicate overlapping sessions, or infer qualifying dusk-to-dawn time from a generic evening timestamp.
- The same rule describes a **separate 80-hour refresher course** (25 classroom, 25 field, 10 highway student driving, 20 highway observation), at least two calendar weeks, and two of the highway driving hours between dusk and dawn; determine whether Lone Star is permitted to offer it and what prior qualification conditions apply.
- Rule .0305 describes inspection rights and **three-year** retention of school records. A retention or deletion feature must respect the applicable rules and institutional policies; do not auto-delete older records pending approval.
- Rule .0601 describes required written student contracts for the 160-hour or refresher courses and their mandatory disclosures/charges. Do not auto-label a template as a legally complete contract until approved by school compliance and NCDMV.
- Greensboro's course and school approval, yard/truck acceptance, instructor licensure, accepted curriculum, daily log templates and reported dates remain **owner-provided facts requiring review**. No automation should assert site or instructor approval without supporting records.

## Texas (Mansfield and Dallas campuses)
- Official Texas Workforce Commission rules, Chapter 807, Subchapter Q: https://www.twc.texas.gov/sites/default/files/ogc/docs/rules-chapter-807-career-schools-colleges-twc.pdf
- Section 807.321 describes a **40-hour truck-driver instructor development course** requirement for covered school instructors and requires relevant FMCSA Part 380 and TPR compliance; school officials should confirm licensing scope, current amendments and instructor documentation with TWC.
- Do **not** automatically apply North Carolina's 160-hour category prescription, night-hours requirement, contract wording or renewal dates to the two Texas campuses. Their program approvals and Texas-specific conditions must be separately confirmed and versioned.

## Academy OS control recommendations
1. Store one approved requirement profile per campus/program with jurisdiction, approval authority/date, effective period and supporting license/curriculum evidence. **Default to review pending**, not compliant.
2. Preserve classroom, yard, actual student-operated road driving, highway observation and verified dusk-to-dawn hours as separate recorded categories. Ensure every daily log is auditable with instructor, date, student, vehicle and correction provenance.
3. Record CLP knowledge-test attempts and remediation separately from federal ELDT theory/BTW certification. Do not block a CLP on a fabricated federal theory-first dependency.
4. Enforce manual administrator sign-off before copying completion evidence into the official TPR. Keep an auditable confirmation reference and verify state DMV testing eligibility.
5. Require campus-specific vehicle approval, instructor qualification, records retention and student contract review before marking a student/campus compliant.

Those require school owner/agency review.
