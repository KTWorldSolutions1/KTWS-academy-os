# KTWS Academy OS — the mega platform

## The product promise

One connected record from first inquiry through enrollment, classroom, yard, road, testing, graduation, employment and retention. At any point an authorized employee should be able to answer: **Where is this student today? What are they working on? What is blocking their next step? Who owns that issue? When will it be reviewed?**

The differentiator is coordination with evidence: a rating becomes a follow-up, a follow-up has an owner, a skill has instructor evidence, and a report shows the underlying record. Campus managers and the owner see the whole operating picture within their authorized scope.

## Implemented in this expansion

| Workspace | What employees can do |
|---|---|
| Daily operations | See attendance recording gaps by current classroom/yard/road location, evidence flags, due follow-ups, vehicle deadlines, credential renewals and today's saved bookings. |
| Support cases | Turn an attention item into a saved assignment with category, student/campus link, owner, plan, due date, next review and outcome. Manage existing automatic training tasks instead of duplicating them. |
| Competency matrix | Compare recorded classroom, yard and road skills across students; identify unevaluated skills and graduation evidence gaps. |
| Campus and class capacity | Review active students, open class seats, ready vehicle statuses, high-priority flags and overdue work across the authorized campus scope. |
| Career outcomes | Record applications, interviews, offers, employers, job starts, follow-up dates and staff-reported 30/90-day retention checks. |
| Progress reporting | Preview and download a sponsor/student progress report with attendance, recorded hours, assessments and competency evidence. Instructor notes are opt-in. Internal handoffs are separate. |

These features extend the existing independent employee login, campus access, enrollment/calendar/cohort workflows, training records, document uploads, manual ledger, contracts, messaging drafts and TPR tracking. They use the existing audited record store. They are not predictive AI or automatic certification.

## The next build waves

The following are proposed additions, not shipped capabilities. Provider-dependent work becomes live only after the school connects and verifies the appropriate service.

### 1. Student self-service and admissions website

- Public school website with approved programs, campus selection, start dates, lead forms and tour booking.
- Separate student account with application checklist, upload requests, enrollment status, class schedule and message inbox.
- Signed agreements returned to the same enrollment record after a verified provider callback.
- Student attendance/hours/progress view, assigned study work, quiz attempts and instructor feedback.
- Payment-plan view and receipts after processor reconciliation; never expose internal case notes or another student's information.

**Completion test:** A prospective student applies from the website, books a tour, provides requested documents, signs an agreement and sees their enrolled class without the office retyping their information.

### 2. Admissions revenue operations

- Lead capture from website forms and approved import sources, with duplicate detection and source attribution.
- Assigned sales queues, time-to-first-contact, tours, no-show recovery, follow-up sequences and conversion reporting.
- Waitlists and a staff-reviewed offer of the next suitable class when capacity opens.
- Follow-up cadence templates with consent, opt-outs, quiet-hour rules and provider delivery receipts.
- Clear reasons for lost opportunities and enrollment delays; a returned prospect can resume their record.

**Completion test:** A missed tour creates a rescheduling task, keeps the original appointment history and stops automated follow-up after opt-out or enrollment.

### 3. Instructor shift and resource dispatch

- Daily assignment board for instructor, student group, truck, yard station, route and lesson objective.
- Instructor availability, approved qualifications, substitutions and equipment conflicts.
- Tablet-first check-in, evaluation rubrics, dictation drafts and review before saving notes.
- Shared pre-trip checks, defect reports and service requests; a maintenance hold blocks new assignments until an authorized release.
- Offline capture with encrypted device storage, explicit synchronization status and conflict handling before it is enabled for private records.

**Completion test:** A truck is removed from service, affected lessons are identified, and the manager reassigns them without deleting recorded instruction or double-booking a replacement.

### 4. Individual learning plans and remediation

- Student-specific skill targets and next practice activities based on recorded gaps.
- Remediation cases linked to the exact assessment or evaluation that triggered them.
- Week-one quiz schedule, question versioning, controlled retakes and instructor-reviewed theory completion.
- Re-entry after absence, class transfers, make-up sessions and planned versus earned hours.
- Instructor calibration: common rubrics and sample evidence so “proficient” means the same thing across campuses.

**Completion test:** A failed theory final creates a reviewed study/retake plan; a later passing attempt clears the current retake flag while preserving both attempts and the original support case.

### 5. Sponsor and workforce funding operations

- Funding authorizations with amount, coverage dates, class/student linkage and required reporting milestones.
- Service milestones, invoice eligibility, billable evidence packets and outstanding sponsor receivables.
- Agency contact directory and a minimal sponsor portal limited to specifically authorized students and fields.
- Approved report templates, report delivery history, receipts and requests for corrections.
- Exceptions when funding remains pending, expires, or conflicts with a student's revised class dates.

**Completion test:** A sponsor request produces a staff-reviewed packet from saved evidence, with recipient scope and sharing history recorded. Sensitive identity and screening documents remain separate.

### 6. Finance and business control

- Tuition contracts, deposits, installment schedules, scholarships, adjustments and refunds with approval history.
- Processor-confirmed payments and reconciliation; no “paid” state based only on a button click or an unverified webhook.
- Campus/program revenue and cost reporting, instructor allocation, truck operating costs and cohort contribution margins.
- Sponsor aging, collections work queues and documented resolution of disputed balances.
- Accounting exports with transaction IDs and reconciliation status.

**Completion test:** A payment webhook can be replayed without creating a duplicate receipt; an approved adjustment preserves the previous balance and audit trail.

### 7. Completion, testing and evidence packets

- Testing appointments, eligibility review, attempts, reschedules and examiner/source references.
- Graduation checklist combining training, approved program requirements, signed agreements and school-configured completion evidence.
- State-specific templates and retention rules configured from the school's verified requirements rather than guessed universal rules.
- Auditable TPR submission preparation, submission result tracking and a direct integration only where verified access permits it.
- Reproducible report packets with a report version, preparer, date range and source record references.

**Completion test:** A reviewer can trace a completion packet back to the exact recorded assessments, signed evaluations and hours. No certificate or submission is generated automatically from an AI recommendation.

### 8. Career services and employer partnerships

- Employer profiles, hiring requirements, approved vacancies, recruiting events and application referrals.
- Resume/interview coaching and consented sharing of student-selected information.
- Job-offer and start verification, follow-ups, retention reminders and reasons a placement did not continue.
- Partner/MOU obligations connected to training contracts, recruiting events and renewal tasks.
- Graduate community, alumni check-ins and refresher program inquiries.

**Completion test:** A graduate moves from application to interview to a verified job start, with 30/90-day tasks created and each outcome tied to its source.

### 9. A grounded school copilot

- Ask “What is Jordan working on today?” and receive a dated answer with the relevant daily training record.
- Ask “Which students have graduation gaps?” and receive a scoped evidence list, not an invented pass/fail judgment.
- Prepare an agency response, instructor handoff or follow-up draft for an employee to review.
- Extract candidate document fields into a review queue; never silently overwrite identity, screening, grades or funding information.
- Search approved school policies and cite their version and passage.
- Track model usage/costs, tool permissions, audit logs and unanswered questions.

**Completion test:** The copilot cites the records it used, honors employee/campus permissions and says when evidence is missing. Sending, financial changes, training sign-offs and eligibility decisions remain explicit authorized actions.

### 10. Configurable workflow automation

- A visual rule builder for school-approved triggers, conditions, tasks, escalation timers and reviewed message drafts.
- Examples: unresolved red rating; due permit review; upcoming class with incomplete enrollment files; approaching sponsor deadline; overdue maintenance; graduate retention follow-up.
- Idempotent execution, clear reasons a rule ran, pause controls and retry visibility.
- Human approval gates for consequential changes and external communications.
- Dry-run mode showing affected records before a new rule is enabled.

**Completion test:** An administrator previews an automation, enables it, and sees one correctly scoped task per event even if the worker retries.

### 11. Employee operations and quality management

- Onboarding checklists, role/campus access, qualification evidence, availability and recurring reviews.
- Assigned duties with escalation, supervisor queues and shift handoffs.
- Incident/near-miss reports with factual evidence, restricted access, corrective actions and closure reviews.
- Student feedback, complaints, service recovery and quality-improvement cases.
- Instructor/course consistency checks using transparent recorded measures.

**Completion test:** A credential deadline prompts review and an employee departure removes access while preserving authored training and audit records.

### 12. Owner intelligence across the campus network

- Funnel conversion, enrollment lead time, start-date capacity, attendance recording coverage, training throughput and completion evidence gaps.
- Sponsor revenue exposure, aging balances, equipment availability and unresolved operating issues.
- Graduations, test attempts, verified placements and retention outcomes with explicit denominators and reporting periods.
- Drill-down from every metric to authorized source records; exports reflect the selected campus/program/date scope.
- Forecast scenarios for enrollment, staffing and equipment demand, labeled as estimates and kept separate from recorded results.

**Completion test:** The owner can identify a capacity bottleneck and inspect its source records without a spreadsheet being manually assembled at every campus.

### 13. Platform ownership and future companies

- Separate school-company tenants with their own branding, domains, approved programs, policies, campuses and data.
- Company-level ownership, delegated administrators, clear access boundaries and customer lifecycle management.
- Supported import/export, versioned APIs, documented integration callbacks and a controlled app/plugin layer.
- Role-specific portals: employee, student, sponsor and employer, each with an intentionally limited data projection.
- Backup verification, recovery procedures, deployment health and an operational support console.

**Completion test:** Two separate companies can use the product concurrently without reading, modifying or exporting each other's records. The current owner/campus model is not yet a multi-company SaaS tenancy system.

## Build priority

1. Validate the newly implemented workflows on the live independent deployment with the first real campus and employee roles.
2. Build student self-service and the public admissions website around the existing enrollment records.
3. Connect consent-aware messaging, signing and payment providers with verified callbacks and receipts.
4. Add instructor/resource dispatch and funding milestone billing.
5. Add the grounded copilot and workflow automation once the underlying records and permissions have been exercised.
6. Expand into multi-company licensing with explicit tenant isolation.

The goal is not a long menu of disconnected tools. It is a platform where staff can finish an actual school workflow, see its evidence, and know exactly who handles the next step.
